Welcome to the Summit

Student Innovation Summit 2026 is a national student event that brings together creative learners, educators, industry professionals, and young entrepreneurs. The summit gives students a platform to present ideas, discover useful skills, and build meaningful connections.

Join us for a full day of project exhibitions, practical workshops, expert talks, competitions, networking, and celebration.

Date: Saturday, August 15, 2026
Time: 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM
Venue: Sunrise College Convention Hall, Kathmandu

Register for the summit

What to Expect

Student Project Exhibition

Explore websites, mobile applications, robots, business ideas, research projects, and community solutions created by students.

Learning Workshops

Join beginner-friendly sessions about web development, digital design, entrepreneurship, robotics, and public speaking.

Expert Talks

Learn from professionals who will share career advice, practical experiences, and ideas about the future of technology.

Who Can Join?

The summit welcomes school and college students, teachers, recent graduates, technology enthusiasts, and invited guests. No advanced technical knowledge is required to attend.

Students may register as visitors, project exhibitors, workshop participants, or competition participants.
