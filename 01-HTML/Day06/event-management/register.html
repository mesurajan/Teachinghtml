Before You Register

Registration is free for students and teachers. Complete the form carefully because important event updates will be sent by email.

  • General visitors may attend talks and the exhibition.
  • Workshop participants must select their preferred workshop.
  • Project exhibitors must provide a short project description.
  • Competition participants may register individually or as a team.
